The practical planning date is 21 November 2026; the exact cut-over hour should be confirmed with counsel before it drives a release gate.
- What replaces the saved ESI rules, regulations and schemes on expiry? The EPF side has a visible successor (Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026, plus the 12 per cent re-notification and VISHWAS 2026 issued under it). I found no equivalent successor ESI instrument.

# [r5] synthesis

## assessment

Draft 3 is a strong evidence document with a broken commercial spine, one live legal misstatement, and a newly-closed research gap that converts its biggest acknowledged blind spot into shippable requirements. Round five does three things to it: it destroys the ₹45–50 price ceiling that anchors §16 and §09 (the real mid-market band clears ₹49.90–₹199.98 PEPM at 50 employees, and ₹122–₹350 at 20); it obtains the Keka pricing the document banned as unobtainable, closing §21 item 6; and it fully researches the AI-hiring discrimination question §13.1 called "the largest unexamined substantive legal risk in the entire evidence base," producing five private-employer-binding statutes, a written-justification duty that lands exactly on the 20-employee ICP floor, and a statutory due-diligence defence that is a better positioning anchor than anything currently in §19.

The three reviewers converge on four things, and convergence across an engineer, a CFO and counsel is the strongest signal in the pack: (1) §09's 93–99% margin claim is arithmetically dead and the document already admits it; (2) attended portal filing — the entire positioning of §06 — has no mechanism, no cost model and no legal scoping, and all three flag it independently; (3) the compliance data set is named the moat in three sections and has no schema, no pipeline, no owner and no headcount; (4) the VERIFIED badge does not mean what §00 says it means, which is the one defect that, if found by an outside reader, discredits everything else.

Where the reviewers want a different document — a functional spec with rounding rules and an ER model, or a fundraising memo with an ask and a cap table — decline, but name the downstream artefacts explicitly so the deferral reads as deliberate. The document's job is to be the decision record that survives hostile review and gates the §21 programme. It currently fails that job in seven specific places, all fixable in prose.

The document must not get longer. Four new sections are justified; roughly the same volume must come out of §00, §05, §11, §17, §18 and the duplicated PROVENANCE block. Target: net flat at ~980 lines.

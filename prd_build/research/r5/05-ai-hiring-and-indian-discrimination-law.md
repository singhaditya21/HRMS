### 4. ### 12. [high] DEFENSIBILITY HOOK. RPwD s.90 (Offences by companies) deems every person in charge of and responsible to the company, and the company, guilty, with an express statutory defence: 'Provided that nothing contained in this sub-section shall render any such person liable to any punishment provided in this Act, if he proves that the offence was committed without his knowledge or that he had exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of such offence.' s.90(2) extends liability to any director, manager, secretary or other officer where the offence is attributable to their neglect. The Explanation defines 'company' as 'any body corporate and includes a firm or other association of individuals'. This 'all due diligence' defence is the strongest legal basis in Indian law for building auditable AI-governance records into an HR product.

RE-VERIFIED verbatim including the Explanation, which the prior draft omitted and which matters because it extends s.90 to firms and unincorporated associations, not just companies. The defence is evidentiary: the employer must PROVE diligence, which requires contemporaneous records.
